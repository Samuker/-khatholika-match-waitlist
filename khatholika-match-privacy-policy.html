Privacy Policy & POPIA Declaration

Last updated: September 2026

Khatholika Match ("we," "us," or "the platform") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA"). This policy explains what information we collect, why, and what rights you have over it.

Who is responsible for your information

Khatholika Match is the responsible party for the personal information described in this policy. If you have any questions, requests, or concerns, you can reach us at:

Email: khatholikamatch@gmail.com

What information we collect

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • WhatsApp number
  • Gender (Man or Woman)
  • What you're hoping to find (Friendship, Courtship, Marriage, Sisterhood, or Brotherhood)
  • Your explicit consent, given at the time of submission

Why we collect it

  • To notify you when Khatholika Match launches
  • To personalize your experience and matches once the platform is live
  • To send you occasional updates about the platform's progress

We do not use your information for any purpose beyond what's described here, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Our legal basis for processing

We process your information on the basis of your consent, given explicitly when you submit the waitlist form. You may withdraw this consent at any time — see "Your rights" below.

How we protect your information

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against loss, unauthorized access, and misuse, including relying on providers with industry-standard security practices.

Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • Ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you
  • Request a copy of your personal information
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Object to us processing your information, at any time and free of charge — you may do this by email, WhatsApp, or any other reasonably convenient method
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before you withdrew it
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator if you believe your rights have been infringed

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at khatholikamatch@gmail.com.

Lodging a complaint with the Information Regulator

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you're welcome to raise it with us directly first — but you always have the right to complain to South Africa's Information Regulator:

  • Website: www.inforegulator.org.za
  • Email: POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the platform develops. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent version.
